Turning on the guided camera opens an in-app camera with flash forced on, for controlled image capture — bypassing the device gallery and system camera.

## How it works

- Flash is always on — there's no toggle.
- After the shutter, the photo is shown for review: Retake or Use photo. Nothing is saved until confirmed.
- The gallery and system camera are not offered.

Note: Requires a device with a flash.
