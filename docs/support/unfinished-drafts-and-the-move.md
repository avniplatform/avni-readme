When a user is moved to the new database, any form they started and did not finish is deleted. The app does not warn them.

## Before an organisation moves

Tell the organisation, before its migration date, that field users must finish and save every draft, or discard it. Anything still a draft on the day of the move is lost.

A draft is not a record waiting to be sent. The number on the sync icon does not count drafts, and syncing does not save them.

## If a user says their drafts are gone after the move

They cannot be brought back. Ask the user to fill the form again.

If the user says records they had **saved** are missing, that is a different problem. Escalate to the platform team with a screenshot of the bottom of the **More** screen.
